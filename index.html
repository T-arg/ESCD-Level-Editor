How links work

Drag a door dot onto another room’s door to connect them. Open-door lines are yellow. A link into the exit room is purple; that approach door stays closed, the exit room door stays open. Cyan T = teleport, orange S = switch — drag onto the target room. Click a connected yellow/purple door, T, or S to drop the link and its data.

Floor influence

For the highlighted room (when it has a switch), tick which of its target room’s floor slots the switch toggles.
